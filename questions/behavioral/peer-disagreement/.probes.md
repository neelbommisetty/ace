# Probe Bank

Follow-up questions to pull from when drilling into this story. Hidden
until the debrief — this file is a dotfile precisely so it stays invisible
before then.

1. What was the peer's strongest argument for their position, in their own words?
2. At what point, if any, did you consider looping in a manager — and why didn't you?
3. What did you personally concede or change your mind about during the conversation?
4. How did you two actually leave things afterward — a real agreement, or just a truce?
