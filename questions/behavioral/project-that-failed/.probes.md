# Probe Bank

Follow-up questions to pull from when drilling into this story. Hidden
until the debrief — this file is a dotfile precisely so it stays invisible
before then.

1. What was the concrete cost — a missed date, a broken feature, lost trust — and who felt it?
2. At what point did you realize it was going wrong, and what did you do in that moment?
3. What was your specific decision or action that contributed to the failure?
4. What did you change afterward that you can point to concretely, not just 'I learned to communicate more'?
