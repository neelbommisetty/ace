# Probe Bank

Follow-up questions to pull from when drilling into this story. Hidden
until the debrief — this file is a dotfile precisely so it stays invisible
before then.

1. What information did you not have, specifically, and who could have given it to you but didn't?
2. What was the call you made, and what was the alternative you didn't take?
3. Did you turn out to be right? What happened when the real requirements surfaced?
4. How did you communicate the assumption you were making to the people depending on your work?
