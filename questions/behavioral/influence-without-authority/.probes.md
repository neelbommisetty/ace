# Probe Bank

Follow-up questions to pull from when drilling into this story. Hidden
until the debrief — this file is a dotfile precisely so it stays invisible
before then.

1. What was the other side's strongest objection before you changed their mind?
2. What did you bring to the conversation that a simple request or email couldn't have?
3. Was there a point where it looked like you might fail to convince them? What happened there?
4. How did you know they had genuinely changed their mind rather than just deferring to you?
